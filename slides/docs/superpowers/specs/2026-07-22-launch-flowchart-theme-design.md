# Launch Flowchart Theme Design

## Goal

Make slides 14 and 15 easier to scan without changing their overall structure. The three deliverable cards remain the primary content, while the process below becomes one visually separate system.

## Approved direction

Use the selected dark green process rail on both launch detail slides.

- Keep the existing slide headings, supporting copy, deliverable cards, images, video, and card descriptions.
- Keep the process directly below the three deliverable cards.
- Add a small centered `How it works` label between the deliverables and the process.
- Present the four process steps inside one full-width dark green rounded rail.
- Use four equal segments with subtle separators and small connecting arrows.
- Keep each step number, step title, and short action word.
- Remove every longer process description from these two slides.
- Give the final step a slightly deeper green treatment to reinforce the destination.

## Responsive behavior

On narrow screens, the rail becomes a vertical stack. The segment order remains unchanged and the arrows point downward. The deliverable cards keep their current responsive behavior.

## Accessibility

Keep the process as an ordered list so screen readers preserve the four-step sequence. Decorative arrows remain hidden from assistive technology.

## Verification

- Both launch detail slides use the same dark process-rail theme.
- Neither launch flow contains a process-description element.
- All four step numbers, titles, and action words remain in order.
- The rail matches the width of the deliverable row.
- Desktop and narrow layouts avoid horizontal overflow.
- The existing deck tests and production build pass.
