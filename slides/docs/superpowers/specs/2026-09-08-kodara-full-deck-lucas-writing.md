# Kodara Full-Deck Lucas Writing Pass

## Problem Statement

The 50-slide Kodara webinar contains several generations of copy. The opening and angle lesson already use a direct spoken-sales voice, while later slides still include internal production notes, legalistic constructions, and generic presentation language. The audience should hear one founder voice from the title through the fit assessment.

## Solution

Rewrite and audit every audience-visible string using Lucas Writing as the governing voice. Preserve the approved 50-slide narrative, evidence, health and compliance boundaries, and current visual system. Replace visible production instructions with honest audience-facing language, without inventing proof or commercial terms.

## User Stories

1. As a webinar viewer, I want the presenter to speak to me directly, so that the presentation feels like one coherent explanation.
2. As a health or wellness expert, I want abstract ideas explained through concrete business consequences, so that I can recognize the problem in my own work.
3. As a prospective Kodara client, I want the mechanism explained before the offer, so that I understand why the model could solve the workload problem.
4. As a careful buyer, I want strong claims paired with clear boundaries, so that I can distinguish the offer from hype.
5. As the presenter, I want internal production notes removed from the audience view, so that every visible slide is ready to present.
6. As the business owner, I want unsupported proof, pricing, guarantees, and outcomes omitted or qualified, so that the copy does not manufacture evidence.
7. As a webinar viewer, I want one clear next step, so that the close follows naturally from the presentation.

## Implementation Decisions

- Keep the Rule of One: the expert supplies knowledge and approval, while Kodara builds the searchable, controlled delivery system around it.
- Keep the belief ladder in its current order: recognize the second-business problem, understand the search and AI mechanism, trust Kodara's implementation, then complete the fit assessment.
- Rewrite titles, body copy, labels, captions, proof framing, objections, and the close in a direct spoken register.
- Use repeated nouns, second-person language, concrete business verbs, contrast, and short landing sentences where they improve clarity.
- Keep the existing title, approved stories, one-hour-per-week statement, proposed 30-day launch language, and all compliance boundaries accurate to the supplied source material.
- Convert incomplete proof and guarantee slides into audience-facing standards and proposed terms. Do not invent Leanne's words, results, prices, guarantee details, or customer outcomes.
- Preserve slide IDs, slide count, navigation, presenter synchronization, URL behavior, keyboard controls, and the existing health-deck design system.

## Testing Decisions

- Use the rendered HTML deck as the public seam.
- Extend the existing webinar contract test to verify the 50-slide narrative, key Lucas-style copy across all five acts, removal of visible production notes, and existing claim guardrails.
- Use browser layout measurements and slide screenshots to verify that the revised copy stays inside the fixed 1920 by 1080 stage.

## Out of Scope

- Inventing or approving testimonials, client results, prices, URLs, contract terms, guarantee eligibility, or product capabilities.
- Changing the slide count, story order, visual identity, presenter features, or interaction model.
- Providing legal approval for the final webinar.
