# Kodara Angle Teaching Expansion

## Outcome

Turn the webinar's keyword-research section into a practical angle-creation lesson that gives health and wellness experts a useful takeaway while naturally demonstrating why they may want Kodara to execute the work.

## Audience takeaway

A keyword shows what people already want. An angle gives them a new, credible reason to pay attention to a particular path.

## Narrative

1. Preserve the existing search-intent lesson.
2. Distinguish demand discovery from angle creation.
3. Keep Demand, Expertise, Clarity, and Deliverability as the responsibility gate.
4. Teach the five OLSEN angle filters: Originality, Leveraged authority, Speed, Ease, and Newness.
5. Prioritize Originality, Leveraged authority, and Speed, with conservative explanations for regulated health and wellness offers.
6. Expand the research process to collect, group, separate, generate, score, and choose.
7. Give viewers a simple 1-to-3 scorecard.
8. Use the current webinar title as the worked example, contrasting it with the generic idea of taking health and wellness knowledge online.
9. End with a positioning sentence and a bridge from keyword research to angle creation to the Kodara build.

## Claim boundaries

- Outside evidence may support a premise, but the slide must not imply endorsement.
- Speed means faster insight or first useful progress, not a guaranteed health or business outcome.
- Ease means less friction, not effortless transformation.
- Newness means a fresh frame, not a fabricated discovery.
- The example must not add a revenue promise, a client guarantee, or a timeline claim.
- Do not import the masterclass's health, revenue, advertising, buyer-percentage, government-payment, or inevitability claims.

## Design and behavior

- Preserve the existing health-deck typography, color, radius, and interaction system.
- Keep every new slide legible inside the fixed 1920 by 1080 stage.
- Use distinct editorial layouts for the distinction, filters, scorecard, and worked example.
- Keep the slide panel, presenter view, URL navigation, keyboard controls, and reduced-motion behavior unchanged.

## Verification seam

- `slides/tests/kodara-webinar.test.mjs` verifies the 50-slide order, required teaching language, health-safe claim boundaries, and existing deck controls.
- Browser review verifies fit, hierarchy, overflow, and consistency on the new teaching slides.
