The AI asks 3–30+ targeted questions to understand the prospect's situation. "What is your primary health goal?" "How much weight do you want to lose?" "What is your ideal timeline?" - customized to your specific industry.

They get access to a basic version of your AI with limited features. Premium actions show a paywall. When they click - the AI upgrades them to the full product ($300–$3,000) without any human involvement.
