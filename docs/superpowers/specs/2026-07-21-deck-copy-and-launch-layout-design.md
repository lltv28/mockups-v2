## Layout rules

- Keep the deck at 20 visible slides.
- Do not add, remove, or reorder slides.
- Fit all content inside the existing 16:9 slide frame without clipping or overlap.
- Keep the current Kodara colors, typography, rounded cards, spacing language, and reveal animations.
- Keep the Organic and Paid Ads flowcharts fully visible below their proof content.
- Preserve keyboard navigation, progress, counter behavior, and video modal behavior.

## Out of scope

- New images, illustrations, or generated assets
- Copy changes on other slides
- Changes to Authority Branding
- Changes to the offer, pricing, bonuses, or payment slides
- Changes to the deck's overall navigation or animation system

## Acceptance checks

1. Slide 1 contains the exact phrase “hot-and-ready buyers.”
2. Slide 2's three offer cards have matching shell styling and no highlighted card.
3. Slide 5 contains the two approved outcome-focused descriptors exactly.
4. Slides 15 and 16 show the approved proof content above their respective flowcharts without overlap.
5. The deck still contains 20 visible slides and works through keyboard navigation at the standard desktop preview size.
