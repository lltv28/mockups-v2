# Launch Slides Merge Correction Design

## Goal

Correct the launch section so the current Slides 14 and 15 become one Authority Branding slide, while the current Slide 16 keeps its content and receives larger square proof previews.

This correction supersedes the Slide 14, Slide 15, Slide 16, slide-count, and related out-of-scope sections in `2026-07-21-deck-copy-and-launch-layout-design.md`. The previously approved changes to Slides 1, 2, and 5 remain unchanged.

## Approved structure

### Merged Authority Branding slide

Keep the current Slide 14 heading, supporting sentence, three card titles, and three card descriptions.

Restyle the three cards as equal proof cards with a large square preview above the existing copy:

1. **Personal branded website** uses `website-bonus.png`.
2. **Done-for-you posting** uses `dfy-marketing.png`.
3. **ManyChat comment and DM automation** uses a new ManyChat-style DM conversation preview built inside the slide.

Place the current Slide 15 four-step Organic flowchart directly below these cards. Preserve the four stages and their meaning:

- Create
- Publish
- Engage
- Convert

Remove the current Slide 15 Organic proof video and its three bullet cards. Remove the now-redundant standalone Slide 15 after its flowchart is moved.

### Current Paid Ads slide

Keep the current Slide 16 heading, three proof-card labels, supporting descriptions, proof assets, and Paid Ads flowchart.

Change each proof card to use a full square media area above its copy, matching the older square-preview treatment:

- Ad Creative keeps the existing Paid Ads video.
- Funnel keeps `leanne-landing.jpg`.
- Pipeline Activation keeps `pipeline-activation-email.png`.

The square previews should use the same sizing and card structure as the merged Authority Branding slide. Images and video should remain understandable at normal desktop preview size without becoming short strips.

## Layout direction

Use the approved **Balanced** direction:

- Three equal cards in one row at standard desktop size.
- A large 1:1 preview at the top of every card.
- Existing title and description below each preview.
- The four-step flowchart spans the slide below the cards.
- The heading and supporting sentence remain above the cards.

The flowchart may be made more compact vertically so all content fits comfortably inside the existing 16:9 frame. Card descriptions must remain visible.

## Deck behavior

- The deck changes from 20 visible slides to 19 visible slides.
- Slides after the removed standalone Organic slide shift forward by one position.
- Preserve keyboard navigation, progress tracking, slide counter behavior, reveal animations, and video modal behavior.
- At narrow widths, cards may stack, but each media preview should keep a square shape and all content must remain reachable.

## Out of scope

- Copy changes outside the current Slides 14 through 16.
- Changes to the approved Slide 1, Slide 2, or Slide 5 revisions.
- Changes to pricing, bonuses, payment, or closing slides.
- New external image searches or third-party assets.
- Changes to the deck's navigation or animation system beyond the automatic slide-count adjustment.

## Acceptance checks

1. The current Slide 14 content and current Slide 15 Organic flow appear together on one slide.
2. The merged slide contains three large square visual previews with the approved website, content-library, and ManyChat visuals.
3. The standalone Organic slide, video, and bullet cards are removed.
4. The current Slide 16 keeps its three proof cards and Paid Ads flow, with full square previews replacing the short media strips.
5. The deck contains 19 visible slides with working keyboard navigation, progress tracking, slide counting, and video playback.
6. Both launch slides fit at the standard desktop preview size without clipping or overlap.
